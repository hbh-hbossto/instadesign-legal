Frequently asked questions

How do I redesign a room?

Take or upload a photo of any room, garden, terrace, or home exterior, pick a style (or let the AI choose), and tap generate. You'll get a photorealistic redesign in seconds with a before/after slider.

My generation failed — was I charged a credit?

No. Credits are only used when a design is successfully generated. If a generation fails, check your internet connection and try again.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Home Design Pro to change or cancel. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Pro until the current period ends.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open the app menu and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple account.

What happens to my photos?

Photos are sent over an encrypted connection to our AI providers solely to produce your redesign, and are not used to train models. Your designs are stored on your device. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Designs and history live on your device — deleting the app deletes them. For any other privacy request, email us.
