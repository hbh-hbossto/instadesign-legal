InstaDesign Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

InstaDesign: Home Design AI ("we", "us") is an AI interior and outdoor design app operated by TEK BH. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Photos you submit

When you generate a design, the photo you choose is sent over an encrypted connection to our AI processing providers — fal.ai (image generation) and Google Gemini (image analysis) — solely to produce your redesign. Photos are processed transiently by these providers and are not used to train their models under our service agreements. Generated results are stored on your device; a copy may be cached temporarily on the provider's CDN to deliver the image to you.

We never sell your photos, and no human reviews them.

Account & purchases

InstaDesign uses anonymous authentication (Firebase) — no name or email is required. Subscriptions are processed by Apple and managed via RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and purchase receipts (never payment card details — Apple keeps those).

Analytics & crash data

We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand feature usage (e.g. "a design was generated") and to fix crashes. This data is tied to an anonymous identifier, not your identity. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not use advertising SDKs.

Data retention & deletion

Designs and history live on your device — deleting the app deletes them. To reset your anonymous analytics identifier, delete and reinstall the app. For any privacy request, contact us at hbh@tekbh.com.

Children

InstaDesign is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We'll update this page when the policy changes; material changes will be noted in the app.
